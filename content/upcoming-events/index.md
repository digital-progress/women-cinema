        **Rome · 23 October 2026**
        <br/><br/>

        ![Rome event](featured.jpg)

        <br/>

        On 23 October 2026, the *Women in Italian Film Production: Industrial Histories and Gendered Labour, 1945-1985* research project will come to Rome for a public event at the Casa Internazionale delle Donne.
        <br/><br/>

        The afternoon will bring together researchers, film professionals and participants from different generations to share the project’s findings, explore women’s histories and labour in Italian cinema, and reflect on memory, archives and the future of the audiovisual industry.
        <br/><br/>

        The programme will include panel discussions, audiovisual materials and a moderated dialogue. A networking aperitivo will follow at 7:30 pm.
